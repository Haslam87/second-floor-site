4. Location & Safety

A real disclaimer, because this app tracks real movement

Delos is not a safety or emergency service. Live location sharing (the VIP feature) is an informational convenience between consenting friends. It is not monitored by us, has no emergency response behind it, and should never be relied on as your only safety plan while walking alone. If you're concerned for your safety, contact local emergency services directly.

Use good judgement about your surroundings, weather, and route conditions. Suggested walks, Local Lore and "Where to Walk Next" are suggestions, not verified safe routes; always follow local signage and access rights. GPS accuracy varies with signal, device and environment, so treat distance, pace and route data as a helpful estimate, not a certified measurement.
