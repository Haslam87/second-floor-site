Delos

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Delos is a walking app made by Second Floor Digital ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what we collect when you use Delos, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. It describes what the app actually does, not a generic template.

The short version: Delos exists to make your own walking more rewarding. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't use third-party tracking or analytics. Every piece of location data described below powers a specific feature you can see and control.

1. Information We Collect

What Delos actually stores

Account

Your email address and a sign-in identity. You sign in either with an emailed link or with Sign in with Apple. With Apple, Apple may give us a private relay email address instead of your real one, and the name you share the first time you sign in (saved as your display name, which you can change). Delos doesn't use passwords.

Location

Tracked walks
While you're recording a walk, Delos collects your GPS location, including in the background and while your phone is locked, so it can plot your route, calculate distance and pace, and reveal it on your personal map ("Fog of War"). This needs "Always Allow" location permission. Delos only collects location in the background while a walk is being recorded: from when you tap Start Walk to when you tap End Walk.
Saved routes
The path, timing, altitude and speed readings of every walk you save, so you can view it later, compare it with past walks, match it to Segments and Personal Bests, and create Segments or saved routes from it.
Place names
When you save a walk we look up a readable place name for it (for example "Pittville Park"), and the town and country it was in, for your walk history and Places Walked map.
Local Lore & achievements
While you walk, Delos checks your position against a list of local landmarks and achievement locations on your device, and records which ones you've discovered.
Live position (opt-in)
If you turn on "Share My Live Walks" and "Share My Live Location" in Settings, the fact that you're walking, and if you chose it your current position, is shared only with friends who have marked you as a VIP. Nothing is shared unless you turn it on, and it's deleted when the walk ends.
One-off location checks
A single foreground location check is used to suggest a nearby town to join, to centre the map, and for "Where to Walk Next". These aren't stored as walks.

Privacy Zone is on by default. The start and end of every walk are trimmed within a radius you can choose in Settings (60 metres by default) before saving. The trimmed points are never stored at all, not just hidden, so your front door isn't part of any saved route.

Apple Health (optional)

If you choose to import walks, Delos reads the walking and hiking workouts you select from Apple Health, including their GPS route, so they count towards your Delos stats. Delos does not read heart rate or any other health data, never writes anything to Apple Health, and never uses Apple Health data for advertising or shares it with third parties for their own use. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app.

Photos

An optional profile photo, and optional photos you attach to your own walks. Delos only accesses the camera or photo library when you choose to add one.

Profile & social

Your display name, friend connections, invite codes, VIP choices, blocked users, likes on walks, notifications generated by those interactions, Segments you create, and your Segment times.

Reports

If you report content or another user, we store the report (what was reported, the reason, and any note you add) so we can review it. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

Walk details you add

Walk names and an optional 1–5 "how did that feel" effort rating. The effort rating is your own opinion, not heart rate or medical data.

Weather

Delos looks up the weather for a walk's location and time using Apple's WeatherKit, and stores a simple summary (temperature and conditions) with each walk. Premium subscribers see it on their walks; it's also used for weather-based achievements.

Subscription status

Whether you have Premium, and the subscription history needed to confirm it. We never see or store your payment details.

Preferences

App settings you choose: theme, weekly distance goal, notification and privacy settings, joined towns, and cosmetic choices like your route colour.

2. How It's Shared

Who can see what

Other Delos users

  • Friends (mutual acceptance required): can see your past, completed walks in the Friends feed (route, distance, time, name and photo), your profile (badges, rank, total distance and how much of your town you've explored), and can like your walks.
  • VIP live status/location (opt-in): see "Live position" above.
  • Segment leaderboards (public): if you walk a Segment, your display name, profile photo and time are visible to any signed-in Delos user on that Segment's leaderboard, and Segments you create show your display name. This is the one place Delos data isn't limited to friends, by design, the same way comparable apps like Strava work.

Service providers

Companies that process data on our behalf, strictly to run the app. None of them may use your data for their own purposes:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage provider. This is where your account and walk data lives.
  • MapTiler: supplies the maps you see; receives requests for the map area you're viewing.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): turns a walk's coordinates into a readable place name.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, WeatherKit, Apple Health, notifications and processing Premium purchases.
  • RevenueCat: manages and verifies Premium subscriptions on our behalf. It receives a random Delos account identifier (not your name or email) and your purchase history from Apple; it does not receive your location or walks.
What we don't do: we don't sell your data, we don't run advertising, and we don't use third-party tracking or analytics of any kind.
3. Your Data, Your Control

Deletion & your rights

Deleting a walk

Any walk can be permanently deleted from its own detail screen, any time. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

Exporting a walk

Any walk can be exported as a standard GPX file from its detail screen, for free.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete Account lets you request full account deletion from inside the app. You're signed out immediately, and your account, walks, photos, friend connections and profile data are permanently deleted within 30 days. Segments you created stay available to other walkers, without your name attached.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK and EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain uses of it. You can do most of this directly in the app; for anything else, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

4. Other Things Worth Knowing

Children

Delos is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under 13.

Where data is stored

Your account and walk data are stored on servers in London, UK. Some of our other providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they're bound by appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, database security that limits each account to its own data) to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.

5. Contact

Second Floor Digital is responsible for your data. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to Contact@secondfloordigital.co.uk.
